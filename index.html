How to read the diff

  • −Red lines exist only in the original text: they were removed.
  • +Green lines exist only in the changed text: they were added.
  • abDarker highlights mark the exact words that changed inside a modified line.
  • @@Hunk headers such as @@ -12,7 +12,9 @@ give the starting line and number of lines in each version. Unchanged lines in between are collapsed; click Expand to show them.

Frequently asked questions

How do I compare two texts online?

Paste the original text into the left box and the changed text into the right box. DiffDuo compares them instantly and highlights every difference: removed lines in red, added lines in green, and the exact changed words inside each line.

What is a diff checker?

A diff checker (or diff tool) finds the differences between two versions of a text. It shows which lines were added, deleted or changed, the same way git diff shows changes in source code.

Is my text uploaded to a server?

No. The comparison runs entirely in your browser, so your text and files never leave your device. This makes DiffDuo safe for confidential documents, code and configuration files.

Can I compare two files?

Yes. Click Open or drag and drop a file onto either box. Any plain-text file works, including code, JSON, XML, YAML, CSV, SQL, Markdown, HTML and log files, up to 50 MB each.

How do I ignore whitespace or capitalization differences?

Turn on Ignore whitespace to treat lines as equal when they differ only in spaces, tabs or indentation, and Ignore case to ignore uppercase and lowercase differences.

What is the difference between split and unified view?

Split view shows the two texts side by side, which is easiest to read. Unified view shows a single column with removed lines marked with a minus and added lines marked with a plus, the same format as git diff and patch files.

Can it compare large files?

Yes. DiffDuo compares files with hundreds of thousands of lines in a background thread, so the page stays responsive. Two 4 MB files with 100,000 lines each compare in well under a second.

Is DiffDuo free?

Yes. DiffDuo is completely free, with no sign-up, no limits on the number of comparisons and no ads.
